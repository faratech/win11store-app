Direction B — "Shelves"
Low-fi sketch, not a deliverable
Microsoft-Store-style storefront: no hero, category shelves stacked vertically, each a horizontal row of compact tiles with a "See all" link. Denser, more products above the fold.
For: more products visible without scrolling; familiar "store" feel; scales as the catalog grows.
Against: loses the editorial "why we picked it" voice — reads like any store, and trust is this store's real asset.
